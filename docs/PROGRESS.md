## How we work

- Claude makes every change and explains it. Phil reviews it and makes the commit, using a short message Claude suggests.
- Everything happens on `main`: no step branches, no pull requests.
- Claude can add non-secret values to `.env` by appending, never reading it. Real secrets (API keys, tokens) Phil pastes in himself.
- Each step's summary says what a push will trigger (see Reference below).

## Open questions for Phil

1. **Repo visibility.** Answered 2026-11-09: public is fine for now (it counts towards Phil's GitHub, and nothing in it is secret); it can be made private later. Keep secret-looking strings out of commits (GitGuardian scans every push).
2. **Cloud sync.** `README.md` and `.env` were Windows Cloud Files entries, which suggests a sync app manages (or managed) this folder. If it still does, exclude the folder or move the repo somewhere unsynced.
3. **How long to keep billing records** (BUILD_PLAN Step 13). Answered 2026-11-11 by a documented default, since Phil has no accountant: **7 years from the end of each billing month.** Deleting a business's data keeps its billing record (usage totals, each month's charge, the Direct Debit's history). HMRC's periods: a limited company keeps records 6 years from the end of the financial year they relate to ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/running-a-limited-company/company-and-accounting-records)); a sole trader 5 years after the 31 January deadline for the tax year ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/self-employed-records/how-long-to-keep-your-records)); VAT records at least 6 years ([VAT Notice 700/21](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/record-keeping-for-vat-notice-70021)). Seven years from the month covers each, with a year to spare for a financial year that ends after the month. HMRC asks for longer in some cases (a late return, a compliance check): a reason to keep a business's records past seven years, decided then.
