## Open questions for Phil

1. **Repo visibility.** BUILD_PLAN says private; the GitHub repo is public.
2. **Cloud sync.** `README.md` and `.env` were Windows Cloud Files entries, which suggests a sync app manages (or managed) this folder. If it still does, exclude the folder or move the repo somewhere unsynced.
3. **How long to keep billing records** (BUILD_PLAN Step 13). Deleting a business's data keeps its billing totals (`usage_periods`), with no end date for now. Confirm the period with your accountant; a scheduled job can then delete them when it's up.
4. **A mobile on two businesses** (Step 10 review). Text commands find the business by the sender's mobile, and a mobile registered to two businesses matches neither, so its commands are ignored. That bites a tradesperson with two businesses, and an owner could do it to another business by typing its mobile. Options: confirm a mobile by a code texted to it before saving it (a few pence each time), refuse a mobile another business has (which tells the owner it's in use), or warn on the Account tab. Phil decides.
5. **Withheld callers on the demo line** (Step 9 review). They now share one allowance of 3 calls a day, so dialling 141 can't get round the cap. Alternatives: a separate, larger allowance for them, or a message asking them to call with their number shown. Phil decides.
